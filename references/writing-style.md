# Writing Style Guide

Reference this guide when drafting any written materials on behalf of the candidate: cover letters, thank-you notes, follow-up emails, outreach messages, LinkedIn copy, resume bullets, and positioning statements.

## Punctuation

- **No em dashes (—)**. Use periods, commas, colons, semicolons, or parentheses instead. Restructure sentences if needed rather than substituting a different dash type.

## Tone

- Direct and confident without being arrogant.
- Conversational but professional. Not stiff, not casual.
- Self-aware about gaps; never defensive or apologetic.

## Structure

- Short paragraphs. No paragraph longer than 5-6 sentences.
- Lead with the strongest point, not the preamble.
- End with a clear, forward-looking statement.

## Voice

- First person, active voice.
- Avoid filler phrases ("I believe that," "I am confident that," "I would like to").
- Preserve the candidate's natural speech patterns when possible.
